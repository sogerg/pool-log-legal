PoolLog – Privacy Policy

“PoolLog” app (pool maintenance log), publisher: sogerg. Last updated: September 29, 2026.

In short

PoolLog collects no personal data. The app has no account, no server, no ads and no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

Data you enter

Your pool profile (shape, dimensions, volume, sanitizer), water tests, products (strength, price), products added, reminders and settings are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. They are not sent to anyone, neither to us nor to any third party.

Network

The app works fully offline and makes no network requests. Only links you tap yourself (cited sources, this page) open in your browser.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional, off by default): if you turn reminders on, the app schedules local notifications on your phone (for example “Time to test your water”). They go through no server.
  • No access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Export

“Export tests (CSV)” opens the system share sheet: you choose where the file goes.

Purchases

The current version offers no in-app purchase. If a paid option is added, payment will be handled by Apple's or Google's store, and this page will be updated first.

Deleting your data

Settings → “Erase all my data” removes everything from the device. Uninstalling the app also deletes its data.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data, including theirs.

Contact

For any question, use the developer contact address shown on the app's page in the App Store or Google Play.

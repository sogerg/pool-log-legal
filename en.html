PoolLog – Privacy Policy

“PoolLog” app (pool maintenance log), publisher: sogerg. Last updated: October 13, 2026.

In short

PoolLog collects no personal data. The app has no account, no server, no ads and no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your phone. Only the optional “PoolLog Pro” purchase goes through the store and RevenueCat (see “Purchases”).

Data you enter

Your pool profile (shape, dimensions, volume, sanitizer), water tests, products (strength, price), products added, reminders and settings are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. They are not sent to anyone, neither to us nor to any third party.

Network

The app works offline. The only network requests concern the optional “PoolLog Pro” purchase: checking whether it was bought, showing its price, buying or restoring it (see “Purchases”). Your water tests, pool and products are never sent. Links you tap yourself (cited sources, this page) open in your browser.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional, off by default): if you turn reminders on, the app schedules local notifications on your phone (for example “Time to test your water”). They go through no server.
  • No access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Export

“Export tests (CSV)” opens the system share sheet: you choose where the file goes.

Purchases

“PoolLog Pro” is an optional one-time in-app purchase (no subscription). Payment is handled by Apple's App Store or Google Play; we never see your payment details. To know whether Pro was bought and to restore it, the app uses the RevenueCat service (RevenueCat, Inc.), which receives an anonymous identifier generated by the app and the purchase information passed on by the store (product, date, store country). No name, email address or pool data is sent. RevenueCat's policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Deleting your data

Settings → “Erase all my data” removes everything from the device. Uninstalling the app also deletes its data.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data, including theirs.
